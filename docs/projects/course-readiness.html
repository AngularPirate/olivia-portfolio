Context

The Boise State School of Nursing offers over 100 courses each term across its undergraduate and graduate programs. Each course needs a working Canvas site before the first day of classes, and I am the only staff member responsible for preparing them.

Problem

Course preparation had depended on individual requests arriving on time and being handled one at a time. With a program this size, that approach left room for inconsistency between courses and for last-minute gaps.

Approach

I developed program-specific course standards and a pre-term readiness checklist covering course structure, settings, and required program content.

Course Readiness: Essential Canvas Prep Checklist

Due: Friday before your course begins.

Tech Team Assistance

Request by Fall: May 1 · Spring: December 1 · Summer: April 1. After these deadlines, faculty manage their own course preparation.

  • ExamsEnsure that all exam settings are appropriate, including exceptions for due dates.
  • ExamsFor remote proctored exams utilizing Honorlock, ensure the setup is complete.
  • QuizzesIf utilizing the testing center, quizzes must be in Classic Quiz format, not New Quiz.
  • GroupsVerify that group assignments are designated properly.
  • GroupsEnsure that discussion groups are set up before students post. New students are not automatically added to groups; faculty must periodically check that no student is left out.
  • CalendarAdjust course material dates as needed.
  • RosterAdd or remove faculty from the Canvas course as necessary.

Pre-Semester Faculty Responsibilities

Identified by program leadership as essential before a course opens to students.

  • SyllabusUpdated; file name includes semester and instructor’s name; includes the note on course content and Idaho Law.
  • BiographyReview and update your professional biography. A professional headshot is recommended.
  • Module 1 / Week 1At least the first week’s content is available.
  • Module 1 / Week 1Assignments have due dates and point values. Content can be locked with a future release date if further edits are needed.
  • ReadingsAll required readings are included and accessible.
  • SettingsUse the Link Validator tool in Canvas to ensure no broken content links.
  • SettingsPublish the course site by Friday at midnight.
  • AccessibilityReview the accessibility report and its recommendations for content.

Why the checklist matters

This checklist ensures that your Canvas course is fully prepared before the class starts, preventing technical issues and ensuring a smooth experience for both faculty and students.

Faculty acknowledgment: on submission, faculty confirm the course is prepared and ready for students.

Outcome

Course sites were ready by the start of term, and faculty requests became more predictable.

Reflection

Consistency across courses matters for students who move through a program together. A predictable course layout lets them spend their attention on the content rather than on finding it.
