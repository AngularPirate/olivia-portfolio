# Surface brief: portfolio home and project case studies

Scope: index.html (Experience mode: the work leads) and projects/*.html (case studies, read like course material). Audience: CWI hiring committee skimming around a second-round interview. Job: find real artifacts and measured outcomes fast, and cite them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Campus wayfinding. Olivia's work is making courses navigable, so the site behaves like a well-made university signage system: one clear sign at the top of every page, then plain, orderly directories. It refuses the category default of a creative-portfolio hero with thumbnails, and the editorial cream-serif look.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, near-black ink, one garnet field (#8A1C32) used only as the sign panel at the top of each page. Overpass (a Highway Gothic descendant) for names, headings, labels and nav; Source Serif 4 for reading text. Hairline rules, directory rows with a label column, no cards, no shadows, square corners.

STORY: The visitor learns who Olivia is and where she is headed in one panel, follows her path, then scans four project rows each with its outcome, and opens a case study that shows the artifact and the evidence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (name, nav). Garnet sign panel full width: name at display scale, role line, intro, Boise / email / LinkedIn in a directory row. Below, the start of My path.

FORM: Candidate 5 of 7 (campus wayfinding signage), seed key 5363bf24, degraded roll, no challengers.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: wayfinding between case studies (breadcrumb "you are here" line and previous/next project directory at the foot of each case study).
